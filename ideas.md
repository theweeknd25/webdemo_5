# Chosen Direction — Deccan House

## Design Movement

**Contemporary Indian residential editorialism.** The site will combine the assurance of a well-designed Indian home with the restraint of an architectural magazine. It intentionally avoids both generic portal UI and a blog-like reading experience by centring tangible next steps, place, and conversation.

## Core Principles

1. **A home journey, not a listing directory:** Section flow moves from confidence to discovery to direct action.
2. **Architecture as context:** Photography crops, stone tonalities, and thin structural lines quietly signal property expertise.
3. **Human Indian cues:** Practical signals such as site-visit assistance, Pune micro-markets, and WhatsApp-style immediacy make the experience familiar to Indian buyers.
4. **Quiet conversion:** Interaction is contained in purposeful action points—explore homes, request a callback, plan a site visit—rather than promotional noise.

## Color Philosophy

The dominant canvas is **limewashed white** and **sun-warmed paper**, evoking the clarity of a model apartment before possession. Charcoal gives the copy authority. A saturated **indigo ink** is reserved for decisive moments so inquiry actions stand out without becoming a blue website. A muted terracotta signal brings gentle Pune warmth to labels and linework rather than luxury clichés.

## Layout Paradigm

The page follows an **offset residential walkthrough**: a left-aligned narrative rail meets large right or full-bleed image rooms, then shifts direction like moving through a home. Content blocks are anchored by pin-style location labels and vertical rule lines. No repeated card grid is used for the main storytelling.

## Signature Elements

1. A small **south-west compass tick** in navigation and section labels, symbolising local orientation.
2. Fine indigo **site-plan rules** that join captions, numbers, and action links.
3. A distinct **terracotta enquiry rail** that returns as a subtle conversation marker across the journey.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like opening a thoughtfully designed home: composed, immediate, and reassuring. Property showcases reveal information on hover; location links draw a fine underline; enquiry choices identify the selected need before smoothly moving the visitor to the contact form. No autoplaying carousels, distracting counters, or aggressive pop-ups.

## Animation

At page entry, narrative copy arrives with a restrained 16px vertical lift and a 420ms custom ease, while the hero photograph gently resolves from a clipped frame. On scroll, editorial markers and process steps fade in sequentially at 60ms intervals. Buttons compress subtly on press. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by keeping all content fully visible without movement.

## Typography System

**DM Sans** carries interface copy and labels for confident clarity; **Playfair Display** is used only for high-emotion display phrases, giving the property story a crafted, aspirational note. Headlines use generous letter spacing and asymmetric line lengths. Numerical project data uses DM Sans with tabular figures. The typography is expressive, never overly editorial: body copy stays brief and customer-forward.

## Brand Essence

**Agastya Elite Estate helps Pune homebuyers make their next address feel clear, considered, and personal.**

Personality: **grounded, discerning, attentive**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are certain but warm; CTAs use clear action language; microcopy feels like a capable local advisor, never a sales script.

Example lines:

> “See the address before you commit to the dream.”

> “Tell us what home needs to make room for.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is an **open courtyard monogram**: two offset architectural brackets create a compact ‘A’ silhouette around a central opening. It is a simple, bold graphic symbol rather than a default-font wordmark, used with an uppercase spaced AGASTYA ELITE ESTATE nameplate.

## Signature Brand Color

**Agastya Indigo — #173D6A.** A grounded, decisive blue that resembles architectural drafting ink and acts as the unmistakable action color.

## Style Decisions

- Primary visual atmosphere remains **limewashed white, sun-warmed paper, stone and restrained terracotta**; Agastya Indigo is reserved as architectural ink for calls to action, rules, captions and critical decision moments.
- Pune imagery must focus on **residential neighbourhoods, greenery, balconies, stone textures, streets and lived-in modern homes** rather than generic luxury villas or unrelated landmarks.
- Every major section carries a recognisable Agastya device: the open-courtyard mark, south-west compass tick, indigo site-plan rule or terracotta enquiry rail.
